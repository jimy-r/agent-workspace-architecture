## Protocol

- **Question.** How much of the always-loaded, workspace-authored text in a `<workspace>` session can move to on-demand files while every rule keeps its operative sentence inline, and do the demoted rules still fire?
- **Standing files.** Before any measurement, `tasks/experiments/E5/manifest.py before` writes the sha256 of every standing file in scope to `tasks/experiments/E5/manifest-before.txt`. The scope is `CLAUDE.md`, every file under `.claude/rules/`, every `SKILL.md` under `.claude/skills/`, `.mcp.json`, and the two copies in `tasks/experiments/E5/inputs/`. At the end, `manifest.py after` writes `manifest-after.txt`, and the two files must be byte-identical.
- **Components (H1).** The workspace-authored floor W is the sum of five components, each read from disk.
  1. The project `CLAUDE.md`.
  2. The user-global `CLAUDE.md`, read only from `tasks/experiments/E5/inputs/global-CLAUDE.md.txt`.
  3. The `MEMORY.md` index, read only from `tasks/experiments/E5/inputs/MEMORY.md.txt`.
  4. Each always-loaded rule. A file in `.claude/rules/` is always-loaded unless its frontmatter carries a `paths:` key, the same test `ghost_token_counter.count_always_loaded_rules` applies. Path-scoped rules are listed with their globs and count 0.
  5. Workspace skill descriptions. For each `.claude/skills/*/SKILL.md` whose frontmatter does not set `disable-model-invocation: true`, the roster line `- <name>: <description>`, with the description as `ghost_token_counter.extract_frontmatter_description` returns it.

  The MCP servers in `.mcp.json` are reported per server and kept out of W, because their tool text is written by the server vendors. Their always-on cost is their deferred tool names. The Result gives the names observed in this card agent's own deferred-tool roster, `ghost_token_counter`'s table estimate (`MCP_SERVER_TOOLCOUNTS`, `AVG_DEFERRED_NAME_CHARS`) beside them, and the full-schema estimate if pinned. Nothing outside `<workspace>` is read, so `ghost_token_counter`'s own server discovery and its `baseline` command, which writes its database, are not run.
- **Counting.** Raw tokens come from `ghost_token_counter.count_tokens`, imported and used read-only. It uses tiktoken `cl100k_base` when importable and chars/4 otherwise, and the Result names which one ran.
- **Calibration.** `get_usage` in this run's main session on 2026-09-28 at 20:40 reported the memory-files category at 28,785 tokens, and components 1 to 4 make up that category. The factor c is 28,785 divided by the raw tokens of components 1 to 4. Every component, current and lean, is reported raw and as raw × c, so the factor also absorbs any per-file wrapper text the harness adds. Pairing the 20:40 reading with the files as they stand assumes the two input copies (taken at 21:09) and the other files match their 20:40 state, and the Result lists each file's modification time. Three cross-checks are reported without a verdict: c against `ghost_token_counter.CLAUDE_TOKENIZER_FACTOR`, calibrated component 5 against the `get_usage` skills category (7,531 tokens, which also holds plugin, user-global and built-in skills, so component 5 must come out lower), and the 2026-09-27 readings.
- **Floor.** The measured median first-turn floor is 84,600 tokens (`tasks/scaffold-register.md:12`). The Result states W as a share of it before and after, taking the lean floor as 84,600 minus the calibrated saving.
- **Lean variant.** It is built under `tasks/experiments/E5/lean/` after the current surface is measured. It moves rationale, dated amendment history and worked cases to on-demand files, trims `MEMORY.md` pointers to recall triggers, and drops text that repeats another always-loaded source, such as phrase lists a component-5 skill description already carries. Every rule keeps its operative sentence inline, and every Iron Law, `<HARD-GATE>` convention and credential rule stays inline. Moved text lands verbatim in the on-demand files, so the variant relocates text and deletes none. On adoption, `CLAUDE.lean.md` replaces `CLAUDE.md`, `global-CLAUDE.lean.md` replaces `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, `MEMORY.lean.md.txt` replaces the memory index, each `rules/<name>.lean.md` replaces `.claude/rules/<name>.md`, and the on-demand files go to `Reference/standing/`. One unified diff, `lean/lean-variant.diff`, runs from the current files to the variant. Skill descriptions and `.mcp.json` stay unchanged. The saving from detaching the two `.mcp.json` servers is reported and not applied.
- **Keep list.** Before the variant is written, a script records in `tasks/experiments/E5/keep-list.json` every line of the six current texts that carries `Iron Law`, `Iron rule`, `HARD-GATE`, `<password-manager>` or `credential` (case-insensitive). Each line gets either a key phrase, a verbatim substring stating its rule, or an exemption with the reason it states no rule. The variant passes its integrity check when every key phrase appears verbatim in the lean always-loaded set (the lean texts of components 1 to 4 plus the unchanged component 5).
- **Measuring the variant.** It uses the same counter and the same factor c, with component 5 unchanged. On-demand files count 0 in W, and the Result reports their sizes.
- **Dollars.** The calibrated saving is priced with `scripts/tier_metrics.py` (`FAMILY_RATES`, `CACHE_READ_MULTIPLIER`, `CACHE_WRITE_MULTIPLIER`), imported and never retyped. It is priced once per main-thread session start as a cache write at the main-lane multiplier, on each later main-thread turn as a cache read, and once per general-purpose or researcher spawn as a cache write at the subagent multiplier, for Fable, Opus 5.5 and Sonnet. Tokens are reported beside dollars.
- **H2 design (later night).** A paired golden-set run, lean against current. `scripts/reasoning_golden_run.py` runs the 89 active cases at its defaults (model `claude-opus-5-5`, effort `max`) and k=3, once per arm, with `--no-write` and `--record-out` into `tasks/experiments/E5/`, and both arms start in the same window. `reasoning_golden_run.py compare` on the two records, current as A and lean as B, reports the paired per-case differences, their mean (lean minus current) and the bootstrap 95% interval (10,000 resamples, seed 20260927). The arm-comparable subset is reported beside the full set without a verdict.
- **Arm isolation (later night).** The two arms may differ only in the six standing texts and the presence of the on-demand files. The user picks the mechanism before the run, and the choice changes neither the hypotheses nor the decision rules. Option A gives each arm its own scratch copy of the workspace surface and its own config directory, holding that arm's global `CLAUDE.md`, that arm's memory index and an unchanged copy of the user settings, so hooks and the deny floor apply in both. Option B runs in a window with no other session open, where a script swaps the six texts to the lean versions for the lean arm and restores them afterwards, verified against `manifest-before.txt`. Under either option a validity check runs first. One run per arm records its first-turn context from its transcript's usage record, and the lean arm's must sit below the current arm's by the H1 calibrated saving, within 15%. If it does not, the run is void and nothing is scored.
- **H3 design (later night).** The 15 probes in `tasks/experiments/E5/recall-probes.md`, frozen tonight by the sha256 the Result records. Each probe runs k=3 times per arm in a fresh headless session under the same isolation, read-only (permission mode `plan`, or with the write tools disallowed if plan mode does not run headless), at the golden runner's model and effort. A run passes when its final answer satisfies the probe's pass rule. A probe passes in an arm when 2 or more of its 3 runs pass. Whether the lean arm opened the probe's named file is recorded beside each run and does not bear on the pass. A run refused by a classifier is re-run once, and a second refusal scores as a fail and is counted in the Result.

## Hypotheses

- **H1, size (tonight).** The lean variant cuts the workspace-authored floor W by 30% or more, meaning 1 − W_lean ÷ W_current is 0.30 or higher in calibrated tokens. The change in W's share of the floor, and the cut over components 1 to 4 alone, are reported beside it without a verdict.
- **H2, reasoning (later night).** The paired golden-set difference, lean minus current, stays inside E0a's detectable effect of 0.071 at k=3.
- **H3, recall (later night).** The recall probes match or beat baseline, meaning the lean arm passes at least as many of the 15 probes as the current arm.

## Decision rules

- **H1.** PASS when the cut is 30% or more and the variant passes its integrity check. FAIL when the cut is under 30%. INCONCLUSIVE when a keep-list phrase is missing from the lean always-loaded set, in which case the variant is fixed and re-measured and the Result records the deviation.
- **H2.** PASS when the mean paired difference and the lower end of its 95% interval are both −0.071 or higher. FAIL when the mean is below −0.071. INCONCLUSIVE otherwise.
- **H3.** PASS when the lean arm passes at least as many probes as the current arm, and FAIL otherwise. The pre-registered kill applies per probe whatever the verdict. A demoted rule whose probe fails in the lean arm goes back inline before any adoption. When that probe also fails in the current arm, the Result flags the rule to the user as one that does not fire even inline.
- **What follows.** An H1 FAIL means no later-night run on this variant, and the Result names the largest remaining components for a second cut. An H1 PASS is evidence to schedule the H2 and H3 night. Adoption needs H2 PASS and H3 PASS after any kill-rule restorations, then a diff the user approves. The main thread logs an adoption as a lift in `scripts/_state/learning_ledger.jsonl` and moves the figures into `Reference/token-discipline-measurements.md` and the scaffold register.

**Quality guard.** H2 and H3 are the quality guards. H1 is offline and changes no behaviour.
